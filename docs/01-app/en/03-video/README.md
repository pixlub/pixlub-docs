# Video

The Pixlub video editor and video modules, by category.

Video works in two ways: a module on its own page, for a quick operation (trim, speed up), or the [video editor](./01-video-editor.md) for a real edit. Either way, decoding, rendering and encoding happen in your browser.

- **[Video editor](./01-video-editor.md)** : Edit clips, sounds, texts and subtitles.

## Categories

| Category | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| Transform | Trim a video, change its speed or frame rate. |
| Conversion | Extract a video's audio track. |
| API services | Automatic face blur and subtitles generated from the audio: these two modules send the video or its audio to our servers. |

In the video editor, image modules can also be placed as an **effect** on a clip: blurs, color adjustments, warps… apply frame by frame.

> [!NOTE]
> **Not available through the API**
>
> Video modules run in your browser: they are not exposed by the public API.
