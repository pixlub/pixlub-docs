# Account and credits

What an account changes, how credits and plans work, and where to set up your workspace.

You do not need an account to use local modules. You do for **AI modules**, which use **credits**, and to benefit from your plan limits.

## Plans

| Plan | For whom |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Every lightweight tool, and 30 free credits each month, no credit card. |
| Basic | Exports with no ads or wait, in 4K, video AI modules and API keys. |
| Pro | Unlimited exports, every AI module and the priority queue. |
| Enterprise | A tailored offer, with dedicated support. |

Exact quotas (exports per day, resolution, imported file size, files per batch, AI runs per day) are compared on the [Pricing](https://pixlub.com/en/plans) page. Subscriptions have no commitment.

## Credits

- An **AI module** costs credits on every run: its price shows before running, and nothing leaves without your confirmation.
- Some modules are charged **per file**, others **per minute** (transcription) or **per operation**.
- A run that fails on our side is **refunded** automatically.
- Each charge shows on screen, and a notification warns you as your monthly credits run out.
- **Credit packs** can be bought on top of a subscription, and never expire.

The same credits are used by the [API](../../02-api/en/04-credits.md).

## Settings

The [Settings](https://pixlub.com/en/settings) page has three parts:

| Part | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Profile, preferences, security. |
| Payments | Payment methods, plan management, payment history, credits. |
| Workspace | Local folder, and the **Developer** section for API keys. |

## Local folder

In **Workspace**, choose a folder on your computer. Pixlub can then:

- **save** your [image graph](./02-image/01-image-graph.md) projects there **automatically**;
- **save your exports** there with the dedicated button (the **Export** button always downloads);
- list its projects when the graph opens.

> [!NOTE]
> **Browser permission**
>
> The browser only gives the chosen folder's name, never its full path, and may ask for permission again on a new visit. This feature relies on the File System Access API, available in Chrome, Edge and Chromium browsers.
