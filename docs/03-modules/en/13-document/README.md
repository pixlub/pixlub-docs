# Documents

| Module | Description | API |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Merge PDFs](./docMerge/README.md) | Combines several PDFs into a single file, in the order you choose. | — |
| [Split a PDF](./docSplit/README.md) | Splits a PDF into several files: page by page, in batches, at the marks placed on the preview or by your own ranges. Exported as PDF or images. | — |
| [Organize pages](./docPages/README.md) | Keeps, deletes, reorders, duplicates or rotates the pages of a PDF. | — |
| [Crop a PDF](./docCrop/README.md) | Trims a margin around the pages, without touching their content: the same everywhere, or specific to some pages. | — |
| [Number pages](./docPageNumbers/README.md) | Adds a number to each page, where and how you want it. | — |
| [Watermark](./docWatermark/README.md) | Lays translucent text or an image over the pages, once or as a tiled pattern. | — |
| [Metadata](./docMetadata/README.md) | Reads and edits the title, author, subject and keywords of a PDF, or wipes them. | — |
| [Sign a PDF](./docSign/README.md) | Places your signature, drawn or imported, on the pages you choose. | — |
| [Images to PDF](./docImagesToPdf/README.md) | Combines images into a PDF: lay out your pages, one or more images each, placed wherever you want. | — |
| [PDF to images](./docPdfToImages/README.md) | Exports each page as PNG, JPEG or WebP, at the chosen resolution. | — |
| [PDF to Word](./docPdfToWord/README.md) | Carries the headings, paragraphs and tables of a PDF into an editable Word document. | — |
| [PDF to Excel](./docPdfToExcel/README.md) | Lays out the content of a PDF in a workbook: aligned columns become columns, numbers become numbers. | — |
| [Office to PDF](./docOfficeToPdf/README.md) | Converts a Word, Excel, PowerPoint or OpenDocument file to PDF. | — |
| [HTML to PDF](./docHtmlToPdf/README.md) | Lays out an HTML file and renders it as a PDF, with selectable text. | — |
| [Compress a PDF](./docCompress/README.md) | Shrinks a PDF by re-encoding its images; the text stays sharp and selectable. | — |
| [Repair a PDF](./docRepair/README.md) | Rebuilds a damaged PDF or one that opens badly: object table, catalog and page tree. | — |
| [Protect a PDF](./docProtect/README.md) | Encrypts a PDF with AES-256 and a password, and limits what can be done with it. | — |
| [Unlock a PDF](./docUnlock/README.md) | Removes the password and restrictions of a protected PDF, if you know its password. | — |
| [Redact a PDF](./docRedact/README.md) | Blacks out the chosen words or patterns: the text truly disappears from the page. | — |
| [Extract text (OCR)](./docOcrText/README.md) | Reads the text of a PDF, a scan or a document photo, then exports it as plain text or a searchable PDF. | — |
| [Compare two PDFs](./docCompare/README.md) | Lists the lines added and removed page by page, and flags the pages whose look has changed. | — |
